Vacuum cup lip life and inspection intervals

A cup-life entry tracks inspection intervals, visible wear and replacement triggers for a consumable sealing edge. Service teams use the record to forecast spares and schedule tool changes before a damaged lip causes an unplanned stop.

Inspection notes usually record cuts, hardening, flat spots and contamination, together with the cup part number and installation date.
