VG-4 cup and surface compatibility matrix

Cup selection begins with surface texture, porosity, curvature and the usable sealing area. The VG-4 matrix marks polished metal and sealed polymer panels as routine candidates, while rough cast surfaces require a trial fit. Porous board and highly curved parts are routed to alternative tooling. Toolroom staff add cup material, lip profile and trial-fit notes before changeover.
