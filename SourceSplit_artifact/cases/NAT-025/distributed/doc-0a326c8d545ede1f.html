Why the VG-4 recipe must stay attached to its success figure

Integration questions often start with whether the commissioning figure can travel with a new recipe.

What does the commissioning record report?

The VG-4 commissioning record reports 96% complete pick-transfer-place success, above the 92% comparison reference. The strict below-reference comparison is therefore not met for the reported figure.

Which settings must accompany that percentage?

Keep the rigid workpieces, initial-pose distribution and vacuum setting with the integration specification. Retain the transfer path, speed and final placement tolerance from the locked commissioning recipe. The measurement window is a single commissioning session containing 50–75 initiated handling trials.

When is a handling attempt complete?

Completion requires pickup followed by uninterrupted movement and placement inside the agreed final tolerance. Starts that abort or drop a part remain in the trial population used for the percentage.

Does the comparison certify a changed installation?

No, the reference is not a certification limit. Before using the figure for an altered installation, establish that the recipe still matches the operating conditions attached to the commissioning comparison.
