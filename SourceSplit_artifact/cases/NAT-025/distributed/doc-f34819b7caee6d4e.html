Counting rule

Completion requires the entire handling sequence. Pickup must be followed by uninterrupted transfer and placement inside the final tolerance before an initiated trial enters the completed-placement tally for this session. Both aborted trials and transit drops stay in the initiated-trial total for this session. The uncertainty bound does not move the result across the comparison reference.
