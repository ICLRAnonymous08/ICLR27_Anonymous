Counting attempts without losing the denominator

A reduction sheet should begin with every initiated attempt, then flag its terminal state. Duplicate event messages are reconciled to one attempt; aborted moves and transfers that lose the part remain visible instead of disappearing during cleanup.

Reporting practice

State the completion rule beside the numerator and denominator, and keep the final placement tolerance with the record. The supervisor signs the reduction sheet after duplicate events and terminal states have been reconciled.
