Complete pick-transfer-place success

Complete pick-transfer-place success is the proportion of all initiated handling trials that finish with the workpiece placed inside the stated tolerance. A trial that aborts or loses the workpiece after pick-up remains part of the denominator.

The term is meaningful only with a defined workpiece set, pose distribution, tool setting, path, speed and placement tolerance. Quality teams place the definition beside the recipe identifier and tolerance drawing so reports use the same counting rule.
