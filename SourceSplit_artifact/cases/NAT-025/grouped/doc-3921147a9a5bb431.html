Changeover rhythm and interlock states in automated cells

A production changeover spans tooling, recipe selection, material presentation, interlock checks and a controlled return to automatic operation. Supervisors often track this work separately from normal cycle time because it involves different risks and staffing.

Safety and process interlocks should be reviewed in the state where the next recipe will operate. A changeover checklist typically ends with a controlled dry cycle and an operator handoff.
