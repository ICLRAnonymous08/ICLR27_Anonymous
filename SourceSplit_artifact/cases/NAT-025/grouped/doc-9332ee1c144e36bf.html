Where vacuum handling fits in the finishing cell

Vacuum tooling lets a finishing cell move rigid panels without edge clamps, leaving more of the part accessible to downstream inspection. Application planning considers available sealing area, surface condition, path clearance and the energy used by the vacuum supply.

The choice also affects changeover work: cups, brackets and recipe parameters must follow the next product family. Engineering handover packages therefore pair the layout drawing with cup-selection and changeover instructions.
