RB-9 Cobb Handling and Edge-Wicking Note

Edge transport

Edge wicking describes liquid travelling inward from a cut sheet edge. It concerns the penetration distance or rate along the material, rather than mass uptake per exposed face area.

A supplier note should identify the cut-edge preparation and the direction used to describe travel. Protect labels when liquid contacts the edge and keep sheet orientation visible.

Retain cut-edge orientation and handling notes with the wicking procedure supplied for the material.
