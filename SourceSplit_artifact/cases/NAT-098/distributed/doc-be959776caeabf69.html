RB-9 Reverse-Side Inspection Card

Immersion preparation

This RB-9 card covers full-sheet immersion, with water contacting both faces and all cut edges. There is no surface clamp isolating one exposed area.

Identify the sheet side marks before immersion and provide a handling tray large enough to prevent folding. Inspect edge damage while preserving the specimen label.

  • Keep the immersion tray and sheet-side marks identifiable through handling and disposal.
