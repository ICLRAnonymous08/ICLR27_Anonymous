Recycled Linerboard RB-9 Profile

Linerboard primer

Recycled linerboard includes fibre and surface-treatment features that belong in its material description.

A primer should identify furnish and sizing terminology without replacing the lot specification.

Keep production-lot identity attached when material is cut into test specimens. Identify the sheet side when preparing material for a surface assessment.
