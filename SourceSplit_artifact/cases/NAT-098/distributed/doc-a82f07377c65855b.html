RB-9 Whole-Sheet Immersion Record

Unclamped sheet profile

The RB-9 immersion profile wets both sheet faces and cut edges without a surface clamp. The laboratory configuration uses a supporting tray to handle the whole specimen.

Keep furnish and sizing references attached to the lot label. Identify the handling support and any edge damage before moving the sheet into the tray.

Preserve sheet labels and tray identification through the whole-sheet immersion handling sequence.
