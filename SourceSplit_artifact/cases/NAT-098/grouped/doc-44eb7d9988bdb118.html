Linerboard Water-Resistance Testing

Water qualification

Linerboard water-resistance qualification needs material identity, conditioning and an exposure procedure.

The memo should identify the records that establish each part.

Keep fixture or preparation exceptions visible for review before the qualification file is closed. Retain sheet-side identification through conditioning, cutting and exposure preparation.
