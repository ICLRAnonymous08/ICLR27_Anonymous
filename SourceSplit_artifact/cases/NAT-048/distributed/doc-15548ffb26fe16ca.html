Playback Interruptions and Battery-Test Validity

An interrupted playback run is reviewed before its elapsed time is accepted for product QA.

Troubleshooting sequence

  • Check whether the source application paused or stopped.
  • Review Bluetooth reconnection events and host power settings.
  • Confirm that neither earbud was removed or returned to the case.
  • Identify the first earbud that stopped producing audio.
  • Keep the player and device logs with the run sheet.

Validity decision

QA records the interruption cause and chooses whether the run can continue, must restart, or is closed as invalid.
