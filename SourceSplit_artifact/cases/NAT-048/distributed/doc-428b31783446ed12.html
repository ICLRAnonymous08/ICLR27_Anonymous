Earbud Battery and Charging Care

Good charging habits and clean contacts reduce avoidable battery stress and intermittent charging.

Owner care

  • Keep earbuds and case dry before charging.
  • Wipe contacts with a clean, dry swab.
  • Avoid storing the product fully depleted for long periods.
  • Keep the case away from parked-car heat and direct sun.
  • Use a compatible charging cable and power source.

Long-term storage

Partially charge the product, switch it off where possible and check the battery periodically.
