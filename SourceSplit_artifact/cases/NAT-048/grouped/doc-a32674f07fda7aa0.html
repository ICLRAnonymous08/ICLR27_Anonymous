Playback Time, Standby Time and Charging Time

Battery-use terms

Playback time
Elapsed time while audio is actively reproduced
Standby time
Powered time without continuous audio playback
Charging time
Time required to reach the declared charge state under stated conditions
Case recharge
Energy transfer from the case to one or both earbuds

A published duration should be read with its volume, listening mode, codec, battery state and stop rule.
