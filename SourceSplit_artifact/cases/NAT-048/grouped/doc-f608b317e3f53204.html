Ear-Tip Fit and Acoustic Seal Guide

A stable acoustic seal depends on tip size, insertion angle and the shape of the listener's ear canal.

Fit procedure

  • Choose the smallest tip that seals without discomfort.
  • Insert the earbud and rotate it into its supported position.
  • Run the device fit check where available.
  • Try the adjacent tip size if bass response or noise cancellation is unstable.
  • Clean and dry tips before refitting.

Acoustic effects

A poor seal can reduce low-frequency response and change active-noise-control behavior even when wireless and battery functions are normal.
