Center, Air, Pull-Down, and Cooling-Time Terms

Cooling terms

Air temperature describes the cooling environment, while fruit-centre temperature describes the product location being observed.

Pull-down refers to the cooling process over time.

An extension note should state the temperature location whenever it discusses completion or cooling duration.
