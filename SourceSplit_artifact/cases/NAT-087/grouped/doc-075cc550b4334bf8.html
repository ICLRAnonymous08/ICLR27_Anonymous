SC-8 Passive-Room Handling Guide

Fan-off arrangement

The SC-8 passive-room arrangement leaves forced-air fans off and places cartons on open racks. The handling configuration relies on room circulation instead of a forced-air tunnel.

Keep carton vents clear and retain rack locations with the carton labels. Loose wrapping should be secured without bridging the air gaps between cartons.

  • Return moved cartons to their mapped rack positions, keeping vents and open circulation gaps unobstructed.
