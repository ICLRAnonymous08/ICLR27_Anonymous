SC-8 Carton Airflow Status

Carton airflow

Carton vents need a clear path for forced air during precooling.

Storage advice for SC-8 should identify blocked openings and the relationship between adjacent cartons.

Keep wrapping and pallet arrangement from covering the planned vent route when moving cartons into the cooler.
