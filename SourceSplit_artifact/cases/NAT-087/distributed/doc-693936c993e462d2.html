SC-8 Passive Carton Cooling Primer

Fans stopped

This SC-8 arrangement uses passive room cooling with the forced-air fans stopped. Cartons stand clear of the wall and air moves through the available gaps without a forced-air tunnel.

Keep wrapping clear of vents and sketch the pallet spacing. The primer explains how to preserve that arrangement during handling and avoid blocking the circulation path.

Check carton labels and rack clearances before returning the load to its mapped storage positions.
