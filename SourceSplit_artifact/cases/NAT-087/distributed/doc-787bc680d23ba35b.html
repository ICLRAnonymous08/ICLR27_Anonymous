SC-8 Cooling-System Electricity Log

Electrical channels

Cooling-system electricity consumption is accumulated electrical energy, recorded in kWh. The equipment log identifies the fan and compressor meter channels and their electrical boundaries.

Entry subject
SC-8 Cooling-System Electricity Log

Keep meter replacements, counter resets and clock settings in chronological order. Retain the equipment-channel identifiers when transferring the log so an energy register is not confused with a temperature channel.
