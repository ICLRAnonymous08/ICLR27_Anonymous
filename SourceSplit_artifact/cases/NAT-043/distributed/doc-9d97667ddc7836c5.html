Measuring Points, Depth to Water and Elevation

Groundwater terms

Measuring point
The marked location from which depth is read
Depth to water
Vertical distance from the measuring point to the water surface
Measuring-point elevation
Surveyed elevation of that mark on the chosen datum
Water-level elevation
Measuring-point elevation minus depth to water

Every reported elevation identifies its vertical datum. A depth value should not be compared directly with an elevation value.
