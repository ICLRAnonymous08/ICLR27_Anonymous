GW-11 Access, Lock and Wellhead Status

The GW-11 site note gives field crews the access and protection information needed before a scheduled well visit.

Wellhead condition

Access gate
Locked; combination held by the groundwater field office
Protective casing
Cap seated and lock operable
Concrete apron
Clear of standing water and visible cracking
Identification
GW-11 marker legible from the access path

Field reporting

Damage, evidence of tampering or restricted access is reported before measurement equipment is lowered into the well.
