Groundwater Network Field Operations

Groundwater network operations cover route planning, well protection, manual measurements, logger downloads and field-document custody.

Visit workflow

  • Confirm access with the land contact.
  • Inspect the wellhead before opening the casing.
  • Use clean equipment and document the measuring point.
  • Download instruments before changing any configuration.
  • Secure the cap and lock before leaving the site.

Data handoff

Field sheets and logger files are transferred under the route identifier, with access or equipment issues attached for follow-up.
