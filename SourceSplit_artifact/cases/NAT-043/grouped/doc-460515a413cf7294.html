Electric-Tape Cleaning and Field Preparation

Electric tapes are prepared in the clean-equipment area to prevent residue from being transferred between monitoring wells.

Preparation sequence

  • Inspect the probe, graduations and reel brake.
  • Wash the wetted length with the approved cleaning solution.
  • Rinse with laboratory-grade water and allow excess liquid to drain.
  • Test the light and audible contact indicators.
  • Pack clean gloves and a separate return bag for used equipment.

Field kit

The tape identifier and most recent reference-check date are written on the visit sheet.
