P-6 Worker-Restart Operations Note

Worker restarts

A worker restart can change process identifiers and interrupt an ongoing monitor.

Support staff should trace the worker lifecycle before interpreting a gap.

Reattach monitoring to the intended process boundary and retain the restart time in the support ticket.
