Converting UK Gallons per Minute to Litres

Unit conversion

UK gallons and US gallons use different volume definitions.

A conversion explainer must identify the convention before converting a flow rate to litres per minute.

Keep the original unit label beside the conversion factor and retain the unrounded result until final reporting.
