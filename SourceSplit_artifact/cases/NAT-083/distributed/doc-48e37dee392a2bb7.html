F-9 Closed-Stop Control Configuration

Closed-stop checkout

The F-9 closed-stop checkout locks the control at its shutoff position. It is a service configuration for inspecting the handle stop and regulating component while the outlet is closed.

Identify the installed regulator and inspect the stop for damage before adjustment. Isolate supply pressure when dismantling the handle assembly.

Release the service lock only after handle reassembly, joint inspection and restoration of the control stop.
