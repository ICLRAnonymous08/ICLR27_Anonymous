F-9 Outlet Pressure Status

Pressure design

Outlet pressure depends on the supply arrangement and where it is observed.

The F-9 design note should identify pressure-tap location and operating state.

Keep static and flowing conditions clearly labelled when discussing plumbing service requirements. Locate the pressure observation relative to the shutoff and regulating components.
