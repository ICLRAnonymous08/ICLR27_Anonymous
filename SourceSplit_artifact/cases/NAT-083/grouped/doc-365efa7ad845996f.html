F-9 Part-Open Aerator Service Note

Part-open inspection

The F-9 control is held partly open while staff inspect the aerator for an uneven stream. The handle stays short of its fully open stop throughout the check.

Look for displaced screens and deposits before removing the aerator. Close the supply for dismantling, protect the seating surfaces and retain the component identifier.

Check the refitted joint for leaks before releasing the fitting to ordinary use.
