Energy and Moisture Measurement Checklist for Dryer Batches

Identify the batch electricity meter and retain its start and end readings with the loading record. Check that mass and moisture entries use the same batch identifier.

Record the moisture basis before calculating water removal; wet-basis and dry-basis entries are not interchangeable.
